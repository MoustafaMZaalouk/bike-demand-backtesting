## Interpretation

At one day, LightGBM beats seasonal naive by MAE, but persistence is better.
At seven days, LightGBM is worse than seasonal naive. Poisson is not a
winning replacement for the simple baselines in this experiment either.
Both bootstrap intervals include zero. No reliable superiority claim is made.
The raw quantile bands under-cover badly and need further work before use.
No hourly result is reported: only daily data is used.
